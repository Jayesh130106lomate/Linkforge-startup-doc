## 1. Executive Summary

**LinkForge** is a campaign link management, smart routing, dynamic QR code, and privacy-focused analytics platform. It was built for digital marketing agencies, growth marketers, and online creators who need fast, reliable campaign infrastructure without the baggage of legacy shorteners.

LinkForge is documented as an alternative to fragmented campaign-link tooling, with short links, dynamic QR codes, custom domains, creator bio pages, routing rules, and analytics in one workflow. Performance, competitor behaviour, live feature availability, and regional pricing must be supported by current tests or market research before they are used as public claims.

## 4. Mission, Vision & Operating Principles

### Mission
To help marketing teams, agencies, and creators manage campaign links and QR codes with clear routing behaviour, practical reporting, and data-minimisation practices.

### Vision
To become the preferred, transparent campaign link backbone for marketing agencies across India and emerging global markets.

### Operating Principles

1. **Campaign Clarity**: Every campaign asset should have an identified owner, target destination, clear attribution, and measurable reporting.
2. **Relevant Destinations**: Traffic routing should guide visitors to the right destination based on device, OS, or location, while always preserving an accountable fallback URL.
3. **Evidence Over Noise**: Analytics should support practical marketing decisions. Define measurement, bot-filtering, privacy, and data-quality limits before interpreting results.
4. **Responsible Infrastructure**: Abuse prevention, destination safety review, access control, and auditability are release requirements that need evidence and periodic testing.
